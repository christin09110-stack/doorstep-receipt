The half this app cannot fetch

The camera side of this claim is already here. Doorstep Receipt has been watching the door since you connected it. The carrier's side is the half no consumer app can fetch: carrier tracking APIs authenticate a shipper account, not a recipient, so the only way this app can see what the carrier claimed is for you to hand over the page you are already looking at.

What happens to it

The image or the text goes to a multimodal model on Amazon Bedrock, which transcribes the four fields this app needs and nothing else. You get a screen showing what it read before any claim is created, and you can change every field on it. Nothing is stored until you accept the reading.

If Bedrock is unreachable, pasted text is read instead by this app's own pattern rules, which cover six carrier formats and say so when they cannot manage. A screenshot cannot be read without the model, and the app will tell you that rather than guessing.

What this app will not write

A doorbell camera can show that nothing was recorded at your door during a window of time. It cannot show that a carrier was dishonest. Every sentence this app produces, including the ones a model writes, is checked against guard.py before you see it, and a sentence that states what a person did is refused and replaced in front of you rather than quietly softened.
