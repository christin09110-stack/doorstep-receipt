What the checksums are for, and what they are not for

Recompute a digest and compare it with its line. If they differ, that file has changed since the pack was built. The pack digest covers the list itself, so a line cannot be edited without changing it too.

They do not establish who assembled the pack, and they do not establish that what it says is accurate. This app built it and this app wrote the digests. Treat them as a seal on an envelope, not as a notary's stamp. Any manifest that implies more than that is doing the thing this product refuses to do everywhere else.
